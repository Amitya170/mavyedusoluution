How a project actually moves

Four handoffs, one accountable team

This is the exact path your project takes. Each step has a dedicated owner and a strict deadline before moving to the next.

01

Submit & scope

We extract strings or ingest your files, flag anything ambiguous, and send back a word count, timeline, and fixed quote within one business day.

02

Subject matter expert

Subject matter experts with relevant domain experience are assigned, briefed on your glossary and tone-of-voice, and given read access to your existing translation memory.

03

Review

First-pass translation, independent proofreading by a second linguist, then automated linguistic QA for length, placeholders, and formatting breaks.

04

Deliver & integrate

We deliver files in their original format—or push them directly to your repo or CMS via API—along with a diff report so you can see exactly what changed.

About us

Who we are — and why it matters to your project

Mavy EduSolutions was founded on a simple insight: localization, AI data, and education services all depend on the same thing — native speakers who understand context, not just vocabulary.

We built a single linguist network that serves all three verticals. The same quality system that catches a mistranslated UI string also catches a mislabeled training example or a culturally misaligned assessment question.

We begin every engagement by thoroughly reviewing your source content and codebase. We don't touch a single string until we understand the system it lives in — because a word that's correct in isolation can be wrong in context.
