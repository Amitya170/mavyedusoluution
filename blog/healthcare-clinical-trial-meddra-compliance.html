Dual Independent Forward & Back-Translation Protocol

For patient-reported outcomes (PRO) and clinical consent forms, regulatory authorities (FDA, EMA, NMPA) expect a rigorous 5-stage validation gate:

  1. Forward Translation: Two independent native medical translators generate draft versions.
  2. Reconciliation: A third senior medical linguist harmonizes discrepancies.
  3. Back-Translation: An independent translator blindly renders the harmonized document back into the source language.
  4. Clinical Review: Primary investigators verify conceptual equivalence against study protocols.
  5. Cognitive Debriefing: Pilot patient interviews confirm comprehension across diverse literacy levels.
